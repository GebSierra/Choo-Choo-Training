Why Choo Choo

One sound at a time, the way reading specialists teach it.

Each lesson teaches one letter sound, then puts it to work in real words. No guessing from pictures. When your child holds “mmm” and slides into “a”, they're reading, and the train rolls on.

Our secret

You're the teacher. We hand you the script.

The app never talks over you and never listens to your child. A small “Say this” card shows you what to say, so yours is the voice your child hears learning to read.

Loved by kids, built on research.

Choo Choo Training is a program your kids will love, but it's built on decades of research. We designed the app first to educate, and second to entertain.

Behind each lesson are academic studies from Harvard, the National Institutes of Health, the U.S. Department of Education, and more. “What's an example?” you might ask. Here are five, with links so you can read them yourself.

Phonics taught step by step

The National Reading Panel (2000) found that systematic, explicit phonics beats unsystematic or no phonics (average effect 0.44).

Read the summary

Sounds tied to letters

Across 52 studies, Ehri and colleagues (2001) found that sound awareness helps reading more when children see the letters too.

Read the study

Spelling helps reading

Graham and Santangelo (2014), 53 studies: spelling instruction improved reading and sound awareness.

Read the study

Short sessions, spaced out

Vlach and Sandhofer (2012) confirmed that practice spread over time helps 5- to 7-year-olds remember (tested with science concepts, not reading).

Read the study

Reading apps can help

Kim and colleagues (2021), a Harvard Reach Every Reader review of 36 studies, found reading and math apps help modestly (+0.31). We say modestly because that is what the evidence shows.

Read the review

Questions parents ask

What age is it for?

Children who are starting to learn letter sounds, usually ages 4 to 6, and older children who are stuck on blending.

Is it really free?

The first 5 lessons are always free. The first 100 families get lifetime access.

Does it use the microphone?

No. The app speaks but never listens. You listen to your child.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open it in Chrome or Safari and add it to your home screen.
