# Founding Families: copy drafts

All drafts. Edit freely. `[brackets]` are fill-ins.

## Landing page (choochootraining.com)

**Headline:** Hear your child read you their first word, this week.

**Subhead:** Ten minutes a day with a train that only moves when your child reads the next sound. You sit beside them; the app shows you exactly what to say.

**How it works (3 lines):**
- 🚂 Your child learns one sound at a time, the way reading specialists teach it.
- 🗣️ You say the sounds; the app tells you how. No screens talking over you.
- 🎉 Every sound read moves the train down the line.

**Founding Families block:**
> **Founding Families: [63] of 100 seats left.**
> The first 100 families ride free for life, and get 3 golden tickets for friends.
> Seats close at Winter Break.
>
> [ **Start the first 5 lessons free** ]

Only one button. The Founding offer appears after lesson 5, in the app.
