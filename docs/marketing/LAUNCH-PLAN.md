# Founding Families: launch plan

Marketing only. Nothing in this folder changes the app. Build items are listed at the end for the app session to pick up later.

## Our marketing rules

1. Sell benefits, not features.
2. Scarcity drives demand, and it has to be real.
3. Talk about what it does for them, not what it is.
4. Sell the story, not the product.
5. Sell the cure, not the prevention.
6. Don't order. Show the dream.
7. Two options, not four to six.
8. The product completes their life.
9. A great hook beats great content.
10. Big money is in selling status.

## Before launch: blockers

- **Licensing.** The README says the app contains Mentava and Reading.com material that isn't cleared for sale. Get permission or replace that material before charging anyone, Lifetime codes included.
- **Accounts and codes.** The app is static today. Codes need a small backend (see the technical sketch).
- **Privacy (COPPA).** Collect parent email only. Nothing about the child.

## The offer

| | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Free taste | First 5 lessons, always free | Proof before payment |
| Founding Family | Lifetime access, first **100 families** only, live seat counter | Real scarcity (rule 2) |
| Golden tickets | Each Founding Family gets **3** lifetime tickets to give away | Network spread |
| Deadline | Unused tickets stop working at **Winter Break** | Urgency tied to the school year |
| After launch | Two plans only: **Monthly** and **Lifetime** | Rule 7 |

## The loop

1. **Hook.** The child finishes lesson 5 and has learned m, a, s. Show the parent the in-app prompt (see `COPY.md`).
2. **Share.** The parent posts their honest experience on X, Facebook or Instagram and tags us, with an incentive disclosure.
3. **Public reply + DM.** We reply publicly (social proof) and send their Founding Family code by DM.
4. **Multiply.** The app shows 3 golden tickets: "for your child's best friend's family." Teachers, reading specialists and SLPs who post get a classroom set (10).
5. **Close.** At the deadline, tickets expire and the two normal plans take over.
6. **Reciprocity, 2–3 months later.** Email active families asking for a review. No reward and no conditions.

## Rules we don't break

- The free pass is **never** conditional on an app store review (Apple and Google ban incentivized ratings).
- Incentivized social posts carry a disclosure (FTC): "Shared for a free Founding Family pass 🚂".
- No scientific claims we can't cite. Say: short daily practice, explicit phonics, a grown-up alongside.
- Scarcity numbers are true. When seats are gone, they're gone.
- No children's faces or names needed in posts; suggest parents keep them out.

## Messaging

| Rule | Avoid | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Benefits | "Phonics lessons with letter tracing" | "Your child reads their first word this week" |
| Cure | "Prevent reading gaps" | "When blending finally clicks" |
| Story | "A reading app" | "A train that only moves when your child reads the next sound" |
| Dream CTA | "Sign up now" | "Hear your child read you a bedtime story" |
| Completes life | "Daily lessons" | "10 minutes between dinner and bath" |
| Status | — | Founding Family badge + conductor's hat no later family can get |

**Audiences, in order:** parents of kids who are struggling with blending (cure buyers); kindergarten teachers and SLPs (multipliers); parents of 3–5 year olds (preparation buyers, harder sell).

## Timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Week −4 | Clear licensing; build codes, seat counter, tickets |
| Week −2 | Landing page live with waitlist; recruit 10 friendly families to post first |
| Launch day | Open Founding seats; founder posts the story |
| Weeks 1–6 | Reply to every post within a day; share the best stories |
| Winter Break | Tickets expire; switch to Monthly / Lifetime |
| +2–3 months | Review-request email to active families |

## What we measure

- Lesson 5 completion rate (is the free taste working?)
- Share rate after lesson 5
- Ticket redemption rate (are the 3 tickets spreading?)
- Families still active at 30 and 90 days
- Reviews after the reciprocity email
