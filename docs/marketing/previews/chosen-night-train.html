Why Choo Choo

Questions parents ask

What age is it for?

Children who are starting to learn letter sounds, usually ages 4 to 6, and older children who are stuck on blending.

Is it really free?

The first 5 lessons are always free. The first 100 families get lifetime access.

Does it use the microphone?

No. The app speaks but never listens. You listen to your child.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open it in Chrome or Safari and add it to your home screen.
