# "Say this" bar audit

Every screen below was opened in a browser at 360 x 640 with the default settings plus **Play recorded letter sounds off** (the grown-up says the sounds), so the round card ("Grown-up: Say: ...") shows. Games were played for 3 to 4 rounds, Saying Sounds was stepped through word by word, Practicing Words was played for 3 rounds. The taught letter `a`, which the app draws as a glyph, is written here as plain "a". Generated by `test/say-audit.mjs` (kept as a test tool: `node test/say-audit.mjs 1-25`, then `node test/say-audit.mjs 0-0 stops`; set `AUDIT_OUT=/some/path` for the JSON output).

Columns: **Round card** is the pill at the top of the game ("Say: ..."), empty when the game has none. **Gist** is the collapsed one-line bar (28 characters at most). **Script** is the expanded sheet. "Round 1-2" means the same text on both rounds.

## Findings

### Fixed (in code and data)

- **Doubled letters were stretched twice.** The Saying Sounds script wrote "miss" as `mmmiiissssss` and "buzz" as `b-uuuzzzzzz`. A doubled letter is one sound: now `mmmiiisss` and `b-uuuzzz` (`js/scripts.js` slowSounds).
- **One wording per game.** Green Light and Station Board changed shape from round to round (and lesson to lesson) depending on whether the sound was a vowel ("Tap the light for sss." then "Light for a as in apple."). They now always read "Light for X." and "Find X." (the same short form as Letter Hunt, which lost its "Touch it." tail so it also never changes). Ticket Check lost its "Ask:" prefix ("Which says X?"), Letter Review's gist now reads "Let your child say it first." (it lacked a subject).
- **Scripts that contradicted the screen.** Wagon Parade said "Every tap plays the sound" even when the grown-up says the sounds (the card then shows "Say: sss"); it now says what Green Light says: the tap plays the sound, and if it does not, say the sound shown at the top. Station Board said "then say the sound shown at the top" when a recording plays it; now parallel to Green Light. Letter Hunt and Wagon Parade said "Your child says the sound first" right after telling the grown-up to say the sound; now "says the sound back".
- **Notes on the New Sound screen that named bare letters** (v, z, x, q, i, y lessons: "Let your child say f first, then v") now use the sound notation ("Let your child say fff first, then vvv").
- **Gists without closing punctuation** (Practicing Words, Read It Again, the Story bar) now end with a full stop; Read It Again's gists were rewritten to fit 28 characters.
- **Lesson overview:** Letter Review and Practicing Words both said "Today we'll review"; Practicing Words now says "Today we'll match".
- **Stories:** "mmmm" and "sssss" in the "Your child says:" lines are now "mmm" and "sss" like everywhere else; Story 3's curly apostrophes match Stories 1 and 2.
- **Grownups > Progress:** a finished world with lessons done but no saved date no longer says "No lesson finished here yet".
- **Layout at 360 x 640:** on a Review Stop end screen "Yes, go on" was cut off by the picture and the long "Your child has learned..." message; the picture and message are smaller on short phones now, and the heading no longer leaves "11." alone on a line. The Say this sheet scrolled by 4 px for the longest scripts; it fits now.

### Not changed: for the owner (taste, teaching approach, or a test-pinned wording)

1. **Two ways to write a vowel sound.** The round card says `Say: iii (as in igloo)`; the gist and script say `i as in igloo` (a bare letter a parent may read as its name). check-content pins the second form. Suggestion: `iii as in igloo`, `aaa as in apple` everywhere.
2. **"grown up" vs "grown-up".** The two agreed quiet sentences ("Today we learn a new letter. Your grown up will say its sound." and "Listen to your grown up. Then touch the letter.") are pinned by check-content; everything else says grown-up. Suggestion: hyphenate both and update the test.
3. **The Review Stop tip contradicts the game scripts.** The tip says "Let your child try first. Only if your child misses, say the sound...", but in Letter Hunt, Green Light, Wagon Parade and Station Board the grown-up says the sound as the cue. Suggestion: "This is a review. Let your child do the finding and the saying. If your child misses one, say the sound together, then try again."
4. **The Review Stop end message mixes tenses and claims mastery:** "Your child has learned 25 sounds. Once they master them, they will be able to read words like: ... With these sounds they can read 252 words!" Suggestion: "Your child has learned 25 sounds. Words made only from these sounds include: quiz, quit, fox, mix, zip, zap, box, six. That is 252 words in all."
5. **Saying Sounds gist** is "Stretch: aaat-, then at." for most words but "sssaaammm, then sam." (no "Stretch:") when the word is long. Suggestion: drop "Stretch:" everywhere, or always use "Say: X, then word."
6. **Owner-worded scripts that are pinned by tests:** Track Tracing "Your child can still move on." (suggestion: "You can move on any time."); gist "Draw it, then they try." mixes "they" with "your child" elsewhere.
7. **No-name fallback in the Stories:** "Pip Meets Pip's friend", "Pip looks at Pip's friend and asks". Suggestion: "Pip Meets a New Friend" and "your new friend" (or ask for the name before the first Story).
8. **Welcome text** says "built on decades of research" and "studies from Harvard, the National Institutes of Health, the U.S. Department of Education"; docs/CURRICULUM.md cites Harvard's Reach Every Reader but not the other two. Please keep to what the sources list.
9. **Did you know? tips:** tips 2 and 14 say the same thing (short, frequent practice); tips 7, 11 and 12 write sounds as /m/, /d/, /w/ ("this says /w/"), which a parent may read as the letter name (suggestion: "this says w-").
10. **Placement check results** name bare letters ("The sound t", "Learned m quickly - a good sign", with an em dash); the tests pin them. Suggestion: write them with the sound notation like the lesson overview ("The sound t-").
11. **Station Board words:** "kin", "cob" and "sob" are unusual for ages 4 to 6, and "jab" appears in the lists of three lessons in a row (19, 20, 21); the bank is shared with the placement check, so I left it.
12. **Stories have no Say this bar** (the page is the script); the parent text lives in the Story time card and the page text.
