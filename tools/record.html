Record letter sounds

Use a laptop with Chrome or Edge in a quiet room. Press Record, say the sound, press Stop, listen, then Download. Save each file into assets/audio/sounds/ with the exact name shown. This page asks for the microphone; the reading app itself never does.

The app looks for m.mp3 first and m.webm second. To use your own recording, delete the matching .mp3 from that folder.

This browser cannot record audio here (no microphone access or no MediaRecorder). Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a laptop, from a secure address (https or localhost).
